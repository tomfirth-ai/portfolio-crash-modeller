# Yield from British Government Stock, 10-year nominal par yield, end of month

- **Files**, each with one row per month, written YYYY-MM:
  - `ten_year_par_yield.csv`, with the header `month,ten_year_par_yield`: The 10-year nominal par gilt yield at the end of each month, in percent. The end-of-month yield (series IUMMNPY) from the Bank's Interactive Database, written to 4 decimal places. The Bank drops trailing zeros.
- **Publisher:** Bank of England.
- **Dataset:** Yield from British Government Stock, 10-year nominal par yield, end of month.
- **Where:** https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/boeapps/database/
- **Licence:** Open Government Licence v3.0, https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
- **Attribution:** Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-16. Months from 2026-10 on are left out, because they had not ended.
